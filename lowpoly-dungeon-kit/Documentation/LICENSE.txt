6. AI-ASSISTED CONTENT DISCLOSURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The concept artwork in "refs/" was produced with a generative image model, and
the base meshes in "raw_glb/" were produced by an automated image-to-3D

available, otherwise the kit's offline silhouette-to-SDF reconstruction). All delivered game assets in "exports/" were then
retopologised, UV-unwrapped, texture-baked, LOD-reduced and validated by human-
supervised automated tooling specific to this kit. No third-party copyrighted
